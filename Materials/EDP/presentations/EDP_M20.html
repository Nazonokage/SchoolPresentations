    DEFENSE PREPARATION

    Two essays

    Allow about 15 minutes per essay. Write one or two focused paragraphs each, using your proposed project where possible.

    1. Defend an event flow for your own campus project. Name its producer, payload, two possible reactions and state rule. Trace one accepted action and one rejected action. Explain which reaction runs synchronously and how you will prove that cleanup works. 2. A student reports that one action produced two notifications. Compare duplicate registration with redelivery after a lost acknowledgment. Explain how you would distinguish them using a trace, repair the relevant cause and demonstrate the repair with a repeatable test.

    Suggested score: quiz 20 points plus 5 per essay, 30 total. Keep both essays as rehearsal notes for Module 21. The summative and periodical have separate slots.
