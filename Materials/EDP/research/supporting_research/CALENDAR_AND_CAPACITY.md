## What this means for 23 modules

Exactly two modules in Finals means **21 module days must occur before the Pre-final exam**, in addition to three earlier summative days and the official periodical exams. A 7/7/7/2 split is a content arrangement, but it is not guaranteed to fit this calendar. The Midterm window is particularly short.

The preparation plan therefore retains **23 researched topic units** and places them provisionally as **7 Prelim + 7 Midterm + 7 Pre-final + 2 Finals**, subject to the capacity check. This distribution follows the early foundations and keeps later advanced topics out of Finals, but it still requires additional school-approved teaching dates for some weekday pairs.

For example, a Mon/Thu pattern has 6, 5, and 5 module/studio days under the conservative assumptions: **16**, versus **21 needed** before Finals. A Tue/Fri pattern has 6, 5, and 6: also **17**. All patterns in this table exclude Saturdays. These are illustrations, not an assigned schedule.

When the timetable arrives, choose a documented resolution if capacity is short:

1. Use actual school-approved makeup or additional teaching days, keeping one module per day.
2. If the school counts a structured asynchronous day as an instructional day, schedule it explicitly with a complete activity and feedback. Do not silently replace a face-to-face module with homework.
3. If no additional instructional days are available, reduce the number of taught core modules and retain the remaining researched units as optional reference. Obtain the instructor's decision before changing the requested 23-module count.

Do not solve the gap by putting two modules into one meeting, dropping the summative exams, moving advanced new topics into Finals, or pretending an exam day is a normal lab day.

## Finals protected for individual work

Use the first two available Finals teaching days for M22 and M23. Both are preparation/review presentations, not additional technology lessons and not the final exam itself. Remaining available days support individual project construction, debugging, a mock run, the summative, and feedback. The final practical exam occurs in the official **14–16 April** window, according to the assigned exam timetable.

If Finals has only three ordinary meetings after reserving the summative, two go to modules and one to supervised studio. Project groundwork must therefore begin earlier in the semester; do not promise several in-class project weeks.
