## A. Lesson Preview & Review

Welcome to your mid-term checkpoint! This assessment will test your understanding of the core concepts we've covered so far. Before you jump into the quiz, let's do a rapid-fire recap of the key ideas:

*   **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI):** The multidisciplinary study of how humans interact with computer technology.
*   **Goal-Driven Design:** Focusing on the user's needs first, rather than technological constraints.
*   **Usability:** How easily a user can achieve their goals. It asks, *"Can someone easily use this?"*
*   **The 5 Dimensions:** Words (1D), Visual Representations (2D), Physical Objects/Space (3D), Time (4D), and Behavior (5D).
*   **Cognitive Psychology:** How the mind works. Key concepts include **Mental Models** (user expectations) and **Affordances** (visual cues on how to use something).
*   **UI vs. UX:** **UI** focuses on the visual interface and style. **UX** is the entire holistic experience of using the product.
*   **UI Formats:** Graphical (GUIs), Voice (VUIs), and Gesture-based.
*   **Elements of Design:** The building blocks of visual design: Color, Line, Value, Space, Shape, Form, and Texture.
*   **The Design Process:** Moving from conceptual **Sketches** to structural **Wireframes**, narrative **Storyboards**, and finally, visually complete **Mockups**.

> **Pro Tip:** Read each question carefully. Pay attention to keywords like "visual," "sequential," or "mind" as they are strong clues for specific terminology.

## C. Analogies and Use Cases

### Real-World Context for Test III
In Test III, you are being asked to distinguish between things that are *part* of Human-Computer Interaction and things that are just general technology or content. 

*   **Think of an Interface as a bridge.** A joystick, a QWERTY keyboard, and a VR headset are all physical or digital bridges that allow a human to control a computer. Therefore, they are examples of HCI.
*   **Think of Content as the scenery.** An advertisement (Ads) or a cryptocurrency (Bitcoin) might exist on a screen, but they aren't the *mechanism* of interaction itself. A GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) is the engine inside the computer that makes the graphics work, but it's not the bridge the human touches.

## D. Lesson Wrap-Up / FAQs

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
*   **Confusing Shape and Form:** Remember, Shape is 2D (height and width). Form is 3D (height, width, and depth/volume).
*   **Confusing UI and UX:** If a question asks about "looks or style," it is referring to **UI**. If it asks about the entire process or feeling, it's **UX**.
