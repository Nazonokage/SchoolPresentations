## B. Main Lesson

### Definition of Key Personnel
Normally, key personnel are defined as those people who are essential to carrying out the work of a project, typically those responsible for the design, conduct, and reporting of the research.

However, in a security context, this definition widens. It is not limited to the project or research; it involves the **whole organization**, from the basic tasks of custodial or cleaning services to the topmost position of the organization.

> **Note:** We use these two definitions to cover key personnel in both project-based and widened organizational scopes. It is ideal to associate key personnel with their roles and responsibilities so a security officer (like you in the future) can easily identify the people who can help resolve security issues.

### Key Personnel in IT System Development
For the interior of IT system development, we already know who the key personnel are:

| Role | Primary Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Project Manager** | Manages the whole development process, ensuring it is on track and can reach the goal and deadlines. |
| **Designer** | Designs the structure of the system, from the design of the database, User Interface, Entity relationships, even the flow of the system itself. |
| **Developer** | Codes the system. |
| **Tester** | Tests the system for errors and bugs, creates the test cases. |

**External Key Personnel:**
For the exterior of the IT system development, these are the other key personnel that can be part of the development indirectly:
*   **Owner of the business:** The one who accepted the project to be made and must be delivered to the client.
*   **Client:** The one who provided the project and the details and objectives of the system.

### Key Personnel in the Organization
In the exterior part of IT development, there are more factors that must be considered beyond the immediate project team. Who is responsible if the budget for the project was not received? Who is responsible if the salary is not provided to the development team? This is where the key personnel of the **wider organization** come in.

There are hundreds of key personnel in an organization, but identifying them is essential in securing the organization and its assets and processes. Here are the most general key personnel you can usually find in an organization:

*   **Owner/Stockholders:** Owns the organization/company.
*   **Operation Officers:** Usually the COOs.
*   **IT Security Officers:** Security officers involving IT.
*   **Accounting Manager:** Handles the day-to-day financial operations of the company.
*   **Finance Manager:** Handles the long-term finance and budgeting of the company.
*   **Human Resources Manager:** Handles the manpower of the company.
*   **Janitorial/Custodial Manager:** Handles the cleaning and maintenance of the building.
*   **Physical Security Officers:** Security guards.

### Involvement of the Security Officer
Your role as a security officer is to identify who is responsible if issues arise in an aspect of system development. 

*   **Example Scenario:** The developed system's code was leaked to the internet publicly, meaning anyone can copy it. Who are the key personnel you should ask?
*   **Resolution:** If your answer is the **Developer** and the **Project Manager**, you are correct. But if it wasn't the developer's fault, then it should be escalated to the whole team. If the developer *was* at fault, then the process is accelerated, and perhaps the resolution and recovery are faster.

## C. Analogies and Use Cases

### The "Household" Analogy
Think of a family household.
*   **The Parent (Owner):** Provides the budget and makes the final decisions.
*   **The Tech-Savvy Child (IT Security Officer):** Fixes the Wi-Fi, sets up the smart TV, and manages the streaming subscriptions.
*   **The Handy Person (Janitorial/Physical Security):** Fixes the leaking faucet and ensures the doors are locked at night.
*   **The Other Parent (Finance Manager):** Balances the checkbook and pays the bills.

If the Wi-Fi goes down, the family doesn't call the plumber. They call the Tech-Savvy Child. Identifying the right "key personnel" ensures a faster resolution.

### Real-World Use Case: A Bank Data Breach
A bank experiences a data breach where customer records are leaked.
*   **Developer:** Investigates the code to see if there is an SQL Injection vulnerability.
*   **IT Security Officer:** Leads the forensic investigation and coordinates with law enforcement.
*   **Finance Manager:** Calculates the financial impact of the fines and potential lawsuits.
*   **Human Resources Manager:** Manages the internal communication and potential disciplinary actions if an insider was involved.
*   **Owner/Stockholders:** Make the final call on how to handle the public relations and regulatory notifications.

## D. Lesson Wrap-Up / FAQs

### Key Takeaways
*   **Key Personnel** are individuals essential to the success of a project or the security of an organization.
*   In IT Development, key personnel include the **Project Manager, Designer, Developer, and Tester**.
*   External key personnel include the **Owner of the business** and the **Client**.
*   In the broader organization, key personnel range from **C-suite executives** to **Janitorial and Physical Security** staff.
*   Identifying the correct key personnel for a specific incident speeds up resolution and recovery.
