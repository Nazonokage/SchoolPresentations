## C. Analogies and Use Cases

### The "External Auditor" Analogy
Imagine you are an independent security auditor hired by RTI. You have been given full access to their operations, their infrastructure, and their business model. 
*   **Question 1 & 2** are about understanding their *environment* and *what they own* (the foundation).
*   **Question 3 & 4** are about identifying the *threats* to those things and telling them how to *fix* it.
*   **Question 5 & 6** are about *strategic advice* and your personal *professional judgment* on whether they are a reliable business partner.

### Real-World Use Case: Vendor Due Diligence
The final question in this case study (Question 6) is a direct reflection of real-world business practices. Before a company hires a software development firm (like RTI), they perform a security assessment. If RTI has a poor security posture, hiring them could introduce catastrophic supply chain risks (as we saw with third-party breaches in earlier modules). Your answer here tests your ability to make a practical business decision based on technical security data.

## E. Guided Drills / Code Projects

### The Case Study Analysis Activity
**Instructions:** Read the Rayleonard Tech Inc (RTI) Security Case. Answer the following questions comprehensively. Write your answers in a formal document.

**Part 1: Environmental & Asset Analysis**

**1. What do you think is the main natural disaster that RTI can encounter, and why? And how would you handle this risk of theirs? (10 pts)**
*   *Guidance:* Identify the geographical or environmental context of RTI (if provided in the case study). Explain why this specific disaster is the most likely threat. Propose a specific **Preventive or Corrective Control** (Physical, Technical, or Administrative) to handle it.

**2. List down all potential assets of RTI. Provide at least 5 and describe them. (10 pts)**
*   *Guidance:* Look for tangible (computers, servers, buildings) and intangible (data, reputation, source code, intellectual property) assets. Describe each one and why it is valuable to RTI.

**Part 2: Risk Identification & Recommendation**

**3. List down all possible risks of RTI and explain why. Give at least 10. (20 pts)**
*   *Guidance:* For each risk, state the threat, the vulnerability, and the potential impact. Ensure you cover physical, technical, and administrative risks. 
    *   *Example format:* "Risk 1: Data Breach. Explanation: RTI stores customer data. If they do not encrypt their databases, this data could be stolen..."

**4. What would you recommend for RTI regarding each risk you identified? (20 pts)**
*   *Guidance:* This must map 1:1 with your list in Question 3. For each risk, provide a specific **Security Control Type** (Physical, Technical, Administrative) and **Function** (Preventive, Detective, Corrective).
    *   *Example format:* "Recommendation for Risk 1: Implement a Technical/Preventive Control by installing a Web Application Firewall (WAF)."

**Part 3: Strategic Advice & Judgment**

**5. Aside from the risks and recommendations you provided, what else would you recommend for RTI? (10 pts)**
*   *Guidance:* Think about broader security measures. This could include security awareness training for employees, drafting comprehensive security policies, creating a disaster recovery plan, or hiring a dedicated security officer.

**6. Knowing the current state of RTI, are you willing to hire them for developing your systems? (10 pts)**
*   *Guidance:* Take a stance (Yes or No). Justify your answer by referencing the severity of the risks you found, their current security posture, and the potential supply chain risks of working with them.
