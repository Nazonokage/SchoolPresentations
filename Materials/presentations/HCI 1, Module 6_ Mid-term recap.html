Rapid-fire recap

Nine ideas to know cold

HCI

The multidisciplinary study of how humans interact with computer technology.

Goal-driven design

User needs first, not technological constraints.

Usability

How easily a user can achieve goals. “Can someone easily use this?”

Five dimensions

Words 1D, visuals 2D, physical objects or space 3D, time 4D, behavior 5D.

Cognitive psychology

How the mind works. Mental models are expectations. Affordances are visual cues.

UI vs UX

UI is the visual interface and style. UX is the whole experience.

UI formats

Graphical (GUI), voice (VUI) and gesture-based.

Elements of design

Color, line, value, space, shape, form, texture.

Design process

Sketches, wireframes, storyboards, then mockups.

Pitfalls and clues

Avoid the common traps

Shape vs form

Shape is 2D: height and width. Form is 3D: height, width and depth.

UI vs UX

“Looks or style” means UI. “Entire process or feeling” means UX.

Visual or sequential

Likely a storyboard or visual representations.

Mind or expectation

Almost certainly cognitive psychology or mental models.

True or false

Ask: can a human physically or digitally interact with this? If yes, it is likely HCI.
