Information Assurance and Security · Module 23 · 80 points

Case study analysis: Rayleonard Tech Inc (RTI)

Apply everything in the course as a hired security consultant.

Preview and review

Refresh before you start

The task
No new theory. Read the scenario about Rayleonard Tech Inc (RTI), a technology company with specific security challenges. Analyze its situation, identify assets and risks, and give actionable recommendations.
Review
  • Natural disaster risks and mitigation
  • Asset identification and classification
  • Risk identification, likelihood and impact
  • Control types (physical, technical, administrative)
  • RMF steps
Activity overview

What you submit

Read the RTI case text (provided separately or in your learning materials), then answer six questions in detail in a formal document. Each question is weighted 10 to 20 points.

The report
A comprehensive written report answering all 6 questions, well structured, using course terminology and directly addressing each prompt.
Reminder
The case text is not part of the module file. You cannot answer without reading it. Do not rush.
Part 1 · Environment and assets

Questions 1 and 2

Q1 (10 pts)
The main natural disaster RTI can encounter, why, and how you would handle it. Use the case’s geographic or environmental context; propose a specific preventive or corrective control.
Q2 (10 pts)
List at least 5 potential assets and describe them. Look for tangible (computers, servers, buildings) and intangible (data, reputation, source code, intellectual property) assets, and why each is valuable.
Part 2 · Risks and recommendations

Questions 3 and 4

Q3 (20 pts)
List all possible risks and explain why; at least 10. For each: threat, vulnerability and potential impact; cover physical, technical and administrative risks. Format: “Risk 1: Data breach. Explanation: …”
Q4 (20 pts)
A recommendation for each risk, mapped 1:1 with Q3. Give a control type and function. Format: “Recommendation for Risk 1: Technical / Preventive: install a web application firewall.”
Part 3 · Strategy and judgment

Questions 5 and 6

Q5 (10 pts)
What else would you recommend beyond your risks and recommendations? Think awareness training, comprehensive security policies, a disaster recovery plan, a dedicated security officer.
Q6 (10 pts)
Are you willing to hire RTI to develop your systems? Take a stance, yes or no, and justify it with the severity of the risks, its security posture and supply chain risk.
Analogy and use case

External auditor and vendor due diligence

External auditor
Q1 and Q2 understand the environment and what RTI owns. Q3 and Q4 identify threats and the fixes. Q5 and Q6 are strategic advice and your judgment on whether RTI is a reliable partner.
Vendor due diligence
Before hiring a software firm like RTI, companies assess its security. A poor posture could introduce serious supply chain risks, as with third-party breaches earlier in the course.
Questions answered

Frequently asked

Where is the RTI text?
Alongside the activity sheet or in course materials, or from your teacher.
How many risks in Q3?
At least 10. Listing 10 to 15 is recommended.
Bullets in Q4?
Yes; a list is preferred. Every Q3 risk needs a clear recommendation.
Is Q6 a trick?
No. It is critical thinking: justify your decision with your risk analysis.
