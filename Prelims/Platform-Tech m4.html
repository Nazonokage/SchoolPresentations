What is a Utility Platform?

A software or technology infrastructure that provides various services and functionalities to users. Serves as a centralized hub for accessing and managing different utilities or resources.

💡 Analogy: Parang kuryente o gripo ng tubig — hindi mo kailangang maghukay ng balon o magtayo ng planta. Pitik lang, andiyan na ang serbisyo.
Key trait: Attracts users by providing useful and often free services.
Domains of Utility Platforms

Cloud & Energy

☁️ Cloud Computing

Resources like computing power, storage, and networking on-demand.

Examples: AWS, Azure, GCP

⚡ Energy Management

Monitor, control, and optimize energy consumption.

Examples: Smart grids, sensors, real-time analytics
Domains of Utility Platforms

IoT & Smart Cities

📡 Internet of Things (IoT)

Connect, manage, and control a network of IoT devices.

Features: Device provisioning, remote monitoring, data collection

🏙️ Smart Cities

Centralized system for managing urban services like transportation, utilities, and civic services.

Domains of Utility Platforms

Data & Communication

📊 Data and Analytics

Tools for processing, analyzing, and visualizing data.

Features: Data integration, ML algorithms, reporting

💬 Communication & Collaboration

Messaging, video conferencing, document sharing, task management.

Examples: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace
Domains of Utility Platforms

Financial Utility Platforms

Provide financial services and infrastructure to businesses and individuals.

  • Payment processing
  • Banking services
  • Financial planning tools
  • Investment platforms
  • Cryptocurrency exchanges

Scenario: Johann's Manufacturing Company

Scenario: Johann owns a large manufacturing company facing challenges with energy consumption and cost reduction. He wants a system that can monitor, analyze, and control energy usage across his facilities.

Question: What kind of utility platform could you implement in Johann's company? Why did you choose that utility platform?

💡 Isipin mo: Aling uri ng utility platform ang tutulong sa pag-monitor at pag-optimize ng energy usage?

👉 Pindutin ang susunod na slide para sa posibleng sagot.

C · Skill‑building · Answer

Johann's Manufacturing — Proposed Solution

✅ Energy Management Utility Platform + IoT Infrastructure

Why this platform: Since Johann's factory faces challenges with high energy expenditure, an energy management utility platform provides specialized tools to track power consumption.

  • Utilizes smart grids, real-time sensors, and automated smart controls to pinpoint which machines consume the most power.
  • Built-in data analytics engine allows Johann to view exact diagnostics and automatically scale down utility usage when areas are idle.
  • Effectively drives down overhead costs.
