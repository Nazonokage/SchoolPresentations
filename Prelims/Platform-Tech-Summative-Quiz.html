Recap

Before you check your answers

This assessment traced one continuous thread: from vacuum tubes to cloud computing, the platform keeps getting more abstract while the underlying job — managing hardware, memory, and resources — stays the same.

01 · HISTORY

Five generations of computing, from vacuum tubes and batch systems to ICs, PCs, and mobile devices.

02 · OS ROLE

The operating system as an extended machine and as a resource manager, multiplexing resources in time and space.

03 · HARDWARE

CPU, memory hierarchy, disks, I/O devices, and buses — the physical layer every platform sits on.

04 · INFRASTRUCTURE

Seven components of IT infrastructure, and how eras evolved from mainframes to cloud and mobile.
