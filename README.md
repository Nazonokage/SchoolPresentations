## ⚡ Quick Start

1. **Pick your subject** from the table below.
2. **Click the subject name** — or jump straight to its clean branch.
3. Open what you need:

| File type | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| `.html` | Opens as slides/deck right in your **browser** |
| `.md` | Renders as notes/reviewers/answer keys directly on GitHub |
| `.pdf` / `.docx` / `.pptx` | Click **"View raw"** or **download** to open locally |

No installs. No setup. Just click and study. 🎉

## 💡 Notes & FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Why per-subject branches instead of just folders?</strong></summary>

Branches let you clone or browse just the class you care about, without dragging the entire archive along. `main` stays the single source of truth; branches are clean mirrors that get refreshed as content is added.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I use this to study for finals?</strong></summary>

Yes — that's the whole point. Every subject's `prelims/` folder has compiled reviewers and past exam answer keys. Start there before diving into individual modules.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What do the emoji/status badges mean?</strong></summary>

✅ Ready = fully uploaded and organized. 🚧 In progress = the content exists locally but hasn't been cleaned up and pushed yet.
</details>

- Key files listed in each section are just starting points — browse the full folder or branch for everything.
- This is a personal study archive, not an official course resource. Content quality reflects lecture notes and self-made reviewers, not guaranteed accuracy.
